About This Prototype / 04

PURPOSE & LIMITATIONS

6G-inspired V2X research sandbox

This is an illustrative, reproducible capstone simulator—not a deployment-ready autonomous driving system or a validated 6G radio implementation. The built-in traffic, channel, and sensing models are deliberately simplified.

Use the dashboard to investigate trade-offs under controlled conditions. To make credible claims, run multiple random seeds and report the test configuration, average results, variability and limitations.
